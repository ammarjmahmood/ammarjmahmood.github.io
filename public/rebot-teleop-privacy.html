reBot Teleop Privacy Policy

Last updated October 4, 2026

reBot Teleop is an iPhone app for controlling a reBot robot arm that you own and run on your own network. This policy explains what the app does with information.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer does not receive any data from the app.

Camera

The app uses the camera only through Apple's ARKit to measure how the phone moves and rotates. Camera images are processed on the device and are never recorded, stored or sent anywhere. Only the phone's position and orientation are sent to your robot computer.

Local network

The app connects directly to the robot computer address you enter, on your local network or your own private network such as Tailscale. It sends the phone's position, orientation, gripper setting and control commands, and receives the arm's status. This traffic goes only to the computer you choose and never to the developer or any third party.

Stored on your phone

The app stores the robot computer address and a pairing session token on your phone (the token in the iOS Keychain). You can remove them at any time with Forget this computer in the app's settings, or by deleting the app.

TestFlight

If you install the app through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and feedback you choose to submit with the developer, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
